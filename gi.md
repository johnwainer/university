**Does an Online University Need a CRM to Begin?**

No, a CRM isn't strictly necessary for the absolute beginning stages of a very small or bootstrapped online university. You could manage initial admission leads, marketing, and retention manually using tools like spreadsheets (e.g., Google Sheets), email platforms (e.g., Gmail or Mailchimp free tier), and basic databases. However, for any meaningful growth-especially in an online model where leads come digitally and retention relies on personalized engagement-a CRM is highly recommended from early on. It automates workflows, centralizes data, tracks interactions, and provides analytics to improve conversion rates, reduce dropouts, and scale marketing efforts efficiently. Without one, manual processes become error-prone and time-intensive as enrollments grow, potentially hindering exponential scaling.
